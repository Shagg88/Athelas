# Athelas 1.1.1

Unzip it over your Athelas folder: your settings, saves and achievements are kept.

## New

- **Update from the launcher.** When a new version is out, an **Update** button appears at the top right of the
  launcher. It downloads the new version from GitHub, checks it, installs it over your folder and starts the
  launcher again. Your settings, saves and achievements are kept. Nothing is downloaded until you press it. From
  this version on, you won't need to download updates by hand.

## Fixes

- **The Two Towers: the rare crash a second or two after starting** is fixed at its cause. Two of the game's
  threads could use its built-in frame timer at the same moment, which the original Xbox (one processor core)
  never did. 1.0.1's guard stays as a backstop.
- **The Two Towers: an incomplete copy of the game** (a missing level sound file) now says which file is missing
  instead of crashing.
- **ROTK on PC:** after updating Athelas, the game folder now gets the new ROTK Fix files when you press Play.
  Before, it kept the version it was first set up with.
