# Athelas 1.7.1

Press **Update** in the launcher, or unzip it over your Athelas folder: your settings, saves and achievements are
kept.

## Fixed

- **The Fellowship of the Ring: sounds going quiet** (#30). After breaking enough things (for example in Bag End),
  new sound effects stopped playing. The game's sound clock was stuck, so finished sounds were never let go and the
  game ran out of sound voices. Sounds now keep playing however much you smash.
- **Launcher: restarts itself after an update** from the home screen (it only did from the old details page). This
  takes effect from your next update after 1.7.1.
