# Athelas 1.7.4

Press **Update** in the launcher, or unzip it over your Athelas folder: your settings, saves and achievements are
kept. Each Xbox game builds itself again the first time you press Play (about 30 seconds).

## New in the launcher

- **The Third Age's menu music** plays when its box is in front, read from your own copy of the game, and it is in
  the Minstrel on the Journey page.
- **New backgrounds:** every game's page and box now rotate The Two Towers' gallery (film stills and concept art)
  and The Fellowship of the Ring's loading-screen sketches, from whichever of the two you have. The Xbox Return of the
  King only has four small menu pictures, which looked blurry on a large screen.
- **The Third Age saves a still of each film you watch** (in `TA-Xbox\art`), for its backgrounds later on.

## Fixed

- **The Return of the King (PC): button pictures still white in the combo list and the upgrade shop** (#34). Those
  screens draw text at double brightness, which washed the pictures out; they now keep their colours.
- **The Return of the King (PC): the special ability in Options** (#34) shows the controller's two buttons, like the
  rest of the controller column, instead of "Buttons 7 + 8".
- **The Third Age:** stutter in screens that rewrite their text every frame (the sharper text redrew it each time);
  and a possible crash when closing the game.
- **Settings:** the Vulkan renderer's description now says plainly that it is untested and has no anti-aliasing,
  HDR or upscalers yet.

## Coming soon

- **The Third Age in other languages:** translation subtitles in German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese,
  Polish, Dutch, Swedish and Russian, as the other three Xbox games have them.
