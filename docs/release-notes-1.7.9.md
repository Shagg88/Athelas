# Athelas 1.7.9

Press **Update** in the launcher, or unzip it over your Athelas folder: your settings, saves and achievements are
kept.

## Fixed (from #49, thanks for the detailed report!)

### The Return of the King (PC)

- **Opening logos**: with "Skip opening logos" on they now skip almost at once (the game insisted on showing the EA
  logo for a moment first), without the voice running over the menu.
- **Skipping movies**: one key press or click skips one movie (a click could skip all of them at once). Space and
  WASD skip a movie too.
- **The Windows key works in the game**, and so do screenshot and recording shortcuts (Win+Shift+S, Game Bar,
  ShareX): the game no longer takes the keyboard for itself alone.
- **No flash after minimizing** (Ctrl+Alt+Del): the game stops drawing while it's minimized.
- **Subtitles stay on**: the launcher no longer switches the game's Subtitles option off every time it starts the
  game (and "on" now means on in every language).
- With "Unlock all" on, Helm's Deep only offers the heroes the game itself allows there.

### The launcher

- **Box art** is shown whole, never cropped, and sharper.
- **The Return of the King (PC)** gets a box made from the PC game's own art (there is no PC box art to download).
- The hint for adding a game sits under the Play button.
- **Music**: when none of your games has front-screen music (it comes from The Two Towers, Fellowship and The Third
  Age), the button says so.
