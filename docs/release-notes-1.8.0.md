# Athelas 1.8.0

Press **Update** in the launcher, or unzip it over your Athelas folder: your settings, saves and achievements are
kept.

## New

- **The Third Age: Battle speed** (Gameplay tab, and the in-game menu): 2x, 3x or 4x during battles only;
  exploring, menus and movies stay at normal speed. It is off by default while we make sure every battle is spotted;
  try it and tell us if a battle doesn't speed up or stays fast afterwards. The sound is off while it's sped up.
- **Fast forward on/off** (all four Xbox games): a new Gameplay setting makes F9 turn fast forward on with one press
  and off with the next, instead of holding it. With a controller, **Back + click the right stick** turns it on or
  off. While the game is sped up, ">> 3x" (or the speed you set) shows in the top-right corner.

## Fixed

- **The Return of the King (PC)**: 30 fps is offered on the PC page again, marked "if an event won't trigger", for
  a scripted moment that doesn't happen at higher frame rates (#49).
