# Athelas 1.8.2

Press **Update** in the launcher, or unzip it over your Athelas folder: your settings, saves and achievements are
kept.

## New

- **Launcher**: a **Mods** tab for every game, with the texture packs; the **About** tab is shorter (the licences and
  the launcher's own options are behind buttons).
- **Launcher**: **What's new** shows every release's notes (#51), and an **Exit** button is on the front screen.
- **Launcher**: with a controller, Down on a page's last setting goes to Save (or Library); the Xbox games' tabs are in
  the PC game's order, with Renderer at the top of Graphics.
- **ROTKPlus mod 1.4.0** by brig-dev (#69), still off by default.

## Fixed

- **The Two Towers**: the title screen no longer shakes with FSR (#54).
- **The Two Towers**: Breached Wall plays at 30 fps whatever your frame rate, so the catapults work (#68).
- **The Two Towers**: "No" on the save prompt no longer counts as "Yes" (#56).
- **The Third Age**: characters no longer vanish in the menus; Smooth leaf edges is removed from the Xbox games (#66).
- **Steam Deck / SteamOS / Linux**: the Xbox games find the controller under Proton and Wine (#65). Started from
  Steam with Steam Input, the game uses Steam's controller (your Steam Input layout works) and the Deck's own controls
  aren't read a second time, so one Deck is one player; without Steam the Deck's controls and a Steam Controller are
  read directly. Tip: add Athelas.exe to Steam as a non-Steam game, set it to run with Proton, and leave Steam Input on.
- **Return of the King (PC)**: in borderless mode the game minimizes when the Start menu or taskbar takes the focus (#60).
- **Return of the King (PC)**: softer subtitle outlines (#61); the Perfect Mode ring keeps its shape (#57).
- **Return of the King (PC)**: a controller another player already has is no longer taken (#67, by brig-dev).
