Fonts for The Fellowship of the Ring's Polish and Russian text ([language] text = "pl" / "ru"): the game's own
fonts have no Polish or Cyrillic letters, so these are given to the game in place of fonts\MyriadMM.ttf (body.ttf)
and fonts\neuehuo_.ttf (title.ttf). All are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (the *-OFL.txt files):
  pl/body.ttf, ru/body.ttf  PT Sans Regular (ParaType)
  pl/title.ttf              Uncial Antiqua (Astigmatic)
  ru/title.ttf              Cormorant Unicase SemiBold (Christian Thalmann)
